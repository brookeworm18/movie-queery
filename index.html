Welcome to Movie Queery

Queer cinema is a living, evolving archive of identity, resistance, creativity, and joy. This site exists to celebrate the legacy of canon and help you to explore what’s coming next. Whether you’re a lifelong cinephile or just beginning to discover LGBTQ+ storytelling onscreen, this will help connect you with films, voices, and perspectives that cover a global spectrum of queer stories, from groundbreaking classics to emerging indie gems. This is a place where hidden histories resurface and new narratives take center stage.

Check out our blog dedicated to the latest in queer film. Stay informed with thoughtful coverage of festivals, premieres, industry shifts, and cultural conversations shaping LGBTQ+ cinema today. From deep dives into influential works to quick updates on breaking news, the blog keeps you connected to a vibrant, ever-changing creative landscape.

Beyond discovery, it’s about understanding and engaging. The site offers curated resources that are here to guide you deeper, offering recommendations, thematic collections, and pathways into films you might not encounter elsewhere. Whether you’re seeking stories about trans experience, queer romance across cultures, or experimental storytelling that challenges convention, you’ll find tools here to expand your perspective.

Making queer cinema more accessible, visible, and celebrated has the power to reflect who we are and imagine who we can become. By bringing together discovery, insight, and community, this space aims to honor the richness of queer storytelling and ensure it continues to thrive. It’s also a place to return to again and again as your tastes evolve and new stories emerge. The more you explore, the more connections you’ll find, across cultures, generations, and experiences.

Get started with our API-driven search function to learn more about a specific title, check out a blog post about emerging trends, or go deep with one of the helpful external resources we've identified!
